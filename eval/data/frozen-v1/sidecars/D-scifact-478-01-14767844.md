# Golli protein negatively regulates store depletion-induced calcium influx in T cells.

SciFact corpus doc_id: 14767844

Format: released abstract text, not an original paper PDF. Sentence indices are zero-based.

[0] Calcium influx is crucial for T cell activation and differentiation.

[1] The detailed regulation of this process remains unclear.

[2] We report here that golli protein, an alternatively spliced product of the myelin basic protein gene, plays a critical role in regulating calcium influx in T cells.

[3] Golli-deficient T cells were hyperproliferative and showed enhanced calcium entry upon T cell receptor stimulation.

[4] We further found that golli regulates calcium influx in T cells through the inhibition of the store depletion-induced calcium influx.

[5] Mutation of the myristoylation site on golli disrupted its association with the plasma membrane and reversed its inhibitory action on Ca2+ influx, indicating that membrane association of golli was essential for its inhibitory action.

[6] These results indicate that golli functions in a unique way to regulate T cell activation through a mechanism involving the modulation of the calcium homeostasis.
